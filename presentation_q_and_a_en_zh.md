# Anticipated Q&A: ICEB2026-212

Use these as prepared answers for the discussion session. The English answers are written for oral delivery, usually around 30-60 seconds each. The Chinese text is a meaning-for-meaning translation to help preparation.

## 1. Why did you choose digital games as the context?

English:
We chose digital games because they are interactive hedonic systems, not just static creative products. In many creative-product contexts, AI disclosure may mainly signal reduced human authorship or effort. In games, however, GenAI can also imply dynamic dialogue, adaptive events, or more responsive world behavior during play. That makes games a theoretically useful context for showing that GenAI disclosure can activate both negative and positive inferences.

中文：
我们选择数字游戏，是因为游戏是互动性的享乐系统，而不仅是静态创意产品。在很多创意产品情境中，AI disclosure 可能主要暗示人类作者性或人类努力减少。但在游戏中，GenAI 也可能意味着动态对话、自适应事件或更有响应性的游戏世界。因此，游戏是一个很适合检验双重推断的理论情境，可以说明 GenAI disclosure 不一定只激活负面反应，也可能激活正面反应。

## 2. What is the main contribution beyond existing AI disclosure or algorithm aversion research?

English:
Many studies on AI disclosure emphasize a discounting effect, especially when consumers value human creation, authenticity, or effort. Our contribution is to show that this is only one side of the story in interactive products. We distinguish the presence of disclosure from the stage of GenAI involvement described by the disclosure. We also identify perceived novelty as a positive mechanism that can coexist with effort-based discounting. So the paper moves from a one-directional disclosure effect to a dual-pathway account.

中文：
很多 AI disclosure 或 algorithm aversion 研究强调折价效应，尤其是在消费者重视人类创作、真实性或努力投入的时候。我们的贡献是说明，在互动性产品中，这只是故事的一面。我们区分了 disclosure 是否存在，以及 disclosure 中描述的 GenAI 参与阶段。同时，我们识别了 perceived novelty 这一正向机制，它可以和基于 effort 的负向折价机制共存。因此，本文把 disclosure 从单向效应推进到双路径解释。

## 3. How exactly is "stage of GenAI involvement" different from disclosure timing?

English:
Disclosure timing refers to when the information is shown to the consumer. In our study, that was not the manipulation. Stage of involvement refers to what role GenAI is described as playing in the production or consumption process. Pre-interaction involvement means GenAI helps create content before gameplay starts. During-interaction involvement means GenAI contributes to content generation during gameplay. So the key distinction is not when the label appears, but what kind of AI role the label communicates.

中文：
disclosure timing 指的是信息在什么时候呈现给消费者，而这不是我们操纵的重点。stage of involvement 指的是 GenAI 被描述为在生产或消费过程中扮演什么角色。pre-interaction involvement 表示 GenAI 在游戏开始前帮助创作内容；during-interaction involvement 表示 GenAI 在游戏过程中参与内容生成。因此，关键区别不是标签什么时候出现，而是标签传达了 AI 的哪种角色。

## 4. Could the during-interaction condition simply make the game sound more advanced or higher quality?

English:
That is a very reasonable concern. We interpret during-interaction disclosure as a cue that can make players expect a more dynamic and novel experience. In that sense, it may indeed signal a more advanced capability. But that is part of the theoretical mechanism rather than just a confound: the stage description changes what players infer about the experience. That said, future research could separate stage from perceived technical sophistication or quality more directly, for example by measuring or experimentally controlling perceived quality.

中文：
这是一个很合理的问题。我们把 during-interaction disclosure 理解为一种线索，它会让玩家期待更动态、更有新颖性的体验。从这个意义上说，它确实可能暗示更先进的能力。但这并不完全是混淆因素，而是理论机制的一部分：参与阶段的描述改变了玩家对体验的推断。当然，未来研究可以进一步区分 stage 与 perceived technical sophistication 或 perceived quality，例如直接测量或实验控制 perceived quality。

## 5. Why did you focus on perceived effort and perceived novelty?

English:
We selected these two mechanisms because they capture the central tension in GenAI disclosure for games. Perceived effort represents the negative source-related inference: if AI is involved, consumers may infer less human labor or craft. Perceived novelty represents the positive experience-related inference: if GenAI is involved, consumers may expect something technologically fresh or interactive. Together, these two constructs allow us to explain why disclosure may hurt and help at the same time.

中文：
我们选择 perceived effort 和 perceived novelty，是因为它们捕捉了游戏中 GenAI disclosure 的核心张力。perceived effort 代表负向的来源相关推断：如果 AI 参与，消费者可能会认为人类劳动或匠心投入减少。perceived novelty 代表正向的体验相关推断：如果 GenAI 参与，消费者可能期待一种技术上新鲜或更具互动性的体验。这两个变量结合起来，可以解释为什么 disclosure 可能同时带来负面和正面影响。

## 6. Why does H4 moderate only the novelty path rather than the effort path?

English:
Theoretically, stage of involvement should matter most for experience-related inferences. Whether GenAI is used before gameplay or during gameplay changes what players imagine the game can do during play. That is directly connected to novelty. For perceived effort, both disclosure conditions still signal AI involvement, so both may reduce the perceived amount of human creative labor. We did not rule out possible stage differences in effort, but our theoretical focus was on the stage-contingent novelty route.

中文：
从理论上讲，GenAI 参与阶段最直接影响的是体验相关推断。GenAI 是在游戏前使用还是在游戏过程中使用，会改变玩家对游戏过程中可能发生什么的想象，这与 novelty 直接相关。对于 perceived effort 来说，两种 disclosure 条件都传达了 AI 参与，因此都可能降低人类创作努力的感知。我们并不排除 stage 对 effort 也可能有影响，但本文的理论重点是 stage-contingent novelty route。

## 7. The indirect effect through effort is only marginal. How should we interpret that?

English:
I would interpret it cautiously. H1 and H2 are individually supported: disclosure lowers perceived effort, and perceived effort is positively related to purchase intention. However, the full indirect effect through effort is marginal. This suggests that the effort-discount mechanism exists, but in this game context it is weaker than the novelty mechanism. That is actually consistent with our broader argument that games are not evaluated only as creative outputs; they are also evaluated as interactive experiences.

中文：
我会比较谨慎地解释这个结果。H1 和 H2 分别得到了支持：disclosure 降低 perceived effort，而 perceived effort 正向影响 purchase intention。但是，完整的 effort 间接效应只是边际显著。这说明 effort discount 机制确实存在，但在游戏情境中，它弱于 novelty 机制。这也符合我们的总体观点：游戏不只是作为创意产出被评价，也作为互动体验被评价。

## 8. Why is perceived novelty much stronger than perceived effort in predicting purchase intention?

English:
One interpretation is that in digital games, players place strong weight on expected experience. Novelty is closely tied to whether the game seems fresh, exciting, and worth trying. Perceived effort still matters, but it is a more source-related judgment. For this type of interactive hedonic product, expected experiential value may be more proximal to purchase intention. That is why novelty appears to be the stronger pathway in our data.

中文：
一个解释是，在数字游戏中，玩家非常重视预期体验。novelty 与游戏是否新鲜、是否令人兴奋、是否值得尝试密切相关。perceived effort 仍然重要，但它更多是来源相关判断。对于互动性的享乐产品来说，预期体验价值可能更接近 purchase intention。因此，在我们的数据中，novelty 是更强的路径。

## 9. Why did willingness to pay not show significant condition-level differences?

English:
Willingness to pay is often noisier than purchase intention. In our data, the WTP distribution was highly skewed, and the median was the same across the three conditions. Participants may also have anchored on the displayed price or may have found it difficult to translate attitudes into a monetary amount in a hypothetical scenario. So we treat WTP as supplementary. The purchase-intention results provide the clearer test of the psychological mechanisms.

中文：
willingness to pay 往往比 purchase intention 噪声更大。在我们的数据中，WTP 分布高度偏态，而且三个条件的中位数相同。参与者也可能受页面价格锚定影响，或者在假设情境中很难把态度转化为具体支付金额。因此，我们把 WTP 作为补充结果。purchase intention 更清晰地检验了心理机制。

## 10. How realistic is a scenario-based experiment for game purchasing?

English:
A scenario-based experiment gives us control over the disclosure wording while holding the product information constant. That is important for internal validity. But I agree that it is not the same as observing real marketplace behavior. We see this as an initial causal test of the psychological mechanism. A natural next step would be field experiments, platform data, or experiments with playable demos, where researchers can examine actual clicking, wish-listing, or purchasing behavior.

中文：
基于情境材料的实验能让我们在保持产品信息一致的情况下控制 disclosure wording，这对内部效度很重要。但我同意，它并不等同于真实市场行为。我们把这项研究视为对心理机制的初步因果检验。下一步可以做现场实验、平台数据分析，或者使用可试玩 demo 的实验，从而考察真实点击、加入愿望单或购买行为。

## 11. Does the sample limit the generalizability of the findings?

English:
Yes, to some extent. Our sample consists of online participants with prior game experience, and the study uses one scenario and one focal game type. This gives us a focused test, but it also means that generalizability should be tested further. Future studies could compare different cultural contexts, different levels of gaming experience, and different game genres. I would present our study as evidence for the mechanism, not as the final estimate of the effect in all game markets.

中文：
是的，在一定程度上会限制外部效度。我们的样本是有游戏经验的在线参与者，研究也使用了一个情境和一种特定游戏类型。这使我们能够进行聚焦的机制检验，但也意味着泛化性需要进一步验证。未来研究可以比较不同文化背景、不同游戏经验水平和不同游戏类型。我会把本文结果视为对机制的证据，而不是对所有游戏市场中效应大小的最终估计。

## 12. Could the results depend on the specific game genre you used?

English:
Yes, genre may matter. We used a narrative role-playing game because GenAI involvement in dialogue, story events, and world content is meaningful in that context. In genres where creativity and narrative are less central, the effort and novelty pathways may look different. For example, in competitive games, fairness or performance concerns may become more important. So genre is an important boundary condition for future research.

中文：
是的，游戏类型可能会影响结果。我们使用叙事角色扮演游戏，是因为 GenAI 在对话、故事事件和世界内容中的参与在这一类型中有意义。在创意和叙事不那么核心的类型中，effort 和 novelty 路径可能不同。例如，在竞技类游戏中，公平性或性能问题可能更重要。因此，genre 是未来研究需要关注的重要边界条件。

## 13. Could authenticity, trust, or perceived quality be alternative mediators?

English:
Absolutely. We do not claim that effort and novelty are the only possible mechanisms. Authenticity, trust, perceived quality, realism, or even fairness could also matter. We focused on effort and novelty because they map directly onto the negative source-related inference and the positive experience-related inference in our theory. Future research could build a broader model that includes these additional beliefs and tests their relative importance.

中文：
完全有可能。我们并不认为 effort 和 novelty 是唯一机制。authenticity、trust、perceived quality、realism，甚至 fairness 都可能发挥作用。我们聚焦 effort 和 novelty，是因为它们直接对应本文理论中的负向来源推断和正向体验推断。未来研究可以建立更完整的模型，把这些额外信念纳入进来，并比较它们的相对重要性。

## 14. Is GenAI disclosure always beneficial if it highlights during-gameplay involvement?

English:
No. Our results do not imply that firms should always emphasize GenAI during gameplay. The positive effect depends on whether the disclosure is credible and whether the described AI role is relevant to the product experience. If the disclosure exaggerates the capability, players may feel misled. Also, in some genres, real-time GenAI may raise concerns about quality control, fairness, safety, or consistency. The practical message is not "always promote AI," but "be precise about what AI does and connect it to genuine user value."

中文：
不是。我们的结果并不意味着企业总是应该强调 GenAI 在游戏过程中的参与。正向效果取决于 disclosure 是否可信，以及所描述的 AI 角色是否真正与产品体验相关。如果 disclosure 夸大能力，玩家可能会觉得被误导。另外，在某些游戏类型中，实时 GenAI 可能引发质量控制、公平性、安全性或一致性担忧。因此，实践启示不是“总是宣传 AI”，而是“准确说明 AI 做了什么，并把它与真实用户价值联系起来”。

## 15. What are the ethical implications of your findings?

English:
I think the ethical implication is that disclosure should be transparent and specific. Our findings show that wording matters, which also means firms should be careful not to use vague or inflated AI claims. Good disclosure should help consumers understand the role of GenAI: whether it was used in production, whether it operates during use, and what kind of experience it may affect. Transparency and marketing value should not be treated as opposites.

中文：
我认为伦理启示是，disclosure 应该透明且具体。我们的研究说明措辞会影响消费者理解，这也意味着企业不应使用模糊或夸大的 AI 宣称。好的 disclosure 应该帮助消费者理解 GenAI 的角色：它是在生产中使用，还是在使用过程中运行，以及它可能影响哪类体验。透明性和营销价值不应该被视为对立关系。

## 16. How do you distinguish GenAI disclosure from general technology novelty or AI hype?

English:
This is a good point. GenAI disclosure may partly operate through general AI novelty, especially because GenAI is currently salient. Our stage manipulation helps refine this: both disclosure conditions mention GenAI, but during-interaction involvement generates stronger novelty than pre-interaction involvement. That suggests participants are not responding only to the word "AI"; they are also responding to the described role of AI in gameplay. Still, future research could test whether the effect changes as GenAI becomes more familiar and less novel.

中文：
这是一个很好的问题。GenAI disclosure 的作用可能部分来自一般性的 AI 新鲜感，尤其是在当前 GenAI 很受关注的背景下。我们的 stage 操纵对此做了一定区分：两个 disclosure 条件都提到了 GenAI，但 during-interaction involvement 比 pre-interaction involvement 带来更强 novelty。这说明参与者并不只是对 “AI” 这个词作出反应，也在回应 AI 在 gameplay 中被描述的角色。当然，未来研究可以检验随着 GenAI 变得更熟悉、更不新鲜，这一效应是否会变化。

## 17. What if some consumers dislike AI in creative products altogether?

English:
That heterogeneity is very plausible. Some consumers may have strong negative attitudes toward AI-generated creative content, and for them the effort or authenticity discount may dominate. Others may be more innovation-oriented and respond positively to novelty. In our current study, we controlled for AI familiarity and openness to technology, but we did not fully model consumer segments. A useful next step is to examine individual differences as moderators.

中文：
这种异质性非常可能存在。有些消费者可能强烈反感 AI 参与创意产品，对他们而言，effort 或 authenticity discount 可能占主导。另一些消费者更偏创新导向，可能更积极地回应 novelty。在当前研究中，我们控制了 AI familiarity 和 openness to technology，但没有完整建模消费者分群。下一步很值得把个体差异作为调节变量来研究。

## 18. If you had to summarize the managerial takeaway in one sentence, what would it be?

English:
My one-sentence takeaway would be: GenAI disclosure should not only say that AI was used; it should clearly explain how AI contributes to the user experience. In digital games, that distinction matters because players may interpret GenAI either as reduced human effort or as a source of novel gameplay value.

中文：
如果用一句话概括实践启示，我会说：GenAI disclosure 不应该只是说明使用了 AI，而应该清楚说明 AI 如何贡献于用户体验。在数字游戏中，这一区分很重要，因为玩家可能把 GenAI 理解为人类努力减少，也可能理解为新颖游戏体验的来源。
