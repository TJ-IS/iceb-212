## Slide 1: Not All GenAI Disclosures Are Alike

Approx. time: 30 seconds

English:
Good morning everyone. My name is Fangzhan Lin, and today I will present our study, "Not All GenAI Disclosures Are Alike." The paper examines how disclosure of generative AI involvement shapes players' purchase intention for digital games. The key idea is that disclosure is not just a simple warning label. In games, it can also signal new forms of interaction and novelty, depending on how the involvement of GenAI is described.

中文：
大家好，我是林方展。今天汇报的论文题目是 “Not All GenAI Disclosures Are Alike”。这篇文章关注的是，在数字游戏情境中，披露生成式 AI 参与会如何影响玩家的购买意愿。核心观点是，GenAI disclosure 不只是一个简单的提示标签。在游戏中，它还可能暗示新的互动体验和新颖性，而这种理解取决于披露中如何描述 GenAI 的参与阶段。

## Slide 2: INTRODUCTION

Approx. time: 45 seconds

English:
Let me start with the motivation. GenAI use is becoming increasingly visible in digital games, for example in product descriptions, developer notes, or platform labels. Prior research on AI-generated products often suggests a negative reaction: when people know that AI is involved, they may infer less human labor, less craft, and lower authenticity. But games complicate this prediction. A game is not only a finished creative product; it is also an interactive hedonic system. Players care about how the game was made, but also about what the game can do during play. So the same disclosure may work as both a production cue and an experiential cue.

中文：
先从研究动机讲起。现在玩家越来越容易在数字游戏中看到 GenAI 使用披露，比如产品介绍、开发者说明或平台标签。既有关于 AI 生成产品的研究通常强调负面反应：当消费者知道 AI 参与创作时，可能会推断人类劳动、更少的匠心和更低的真实性。但是游戏这个情境更复杂。游戏不仅是一个已经完成的创意产品，它同时也是一个互动性的享乐系统。玩家既关心游戏是怎么做出来的，也关心游戏在游玩过程中能提供什么体验。因此，同样的 disclosure 既可能是生产来源线索，也可能是体验价值线索。

## Slide 3: Research Question

Approx. time: 30 seconds

English:
This leads to our research question: how does GenAI disclosure influence players' purchase intention for digital games, and through what mechanisms does this effect emerge? We focus on two belief-based mechanisms. One is perceived effort, which captures the human labor and craft that players attribute to the game. The other is perceived novelty, which captures whether the game seems fresh, technologically distinctive, and potentially different from existing games.

中文：
由此引出本文的研究问题：GenAI disclosure 会如何影响玩家对数字游戏的购买意愿？这种影响又是通过什么机制发生的？我们重点考察两个基于信念的机制。第一个是 perceived effort，也就是玩家认为这款游戏包含了多少人类劳动和创作投入。第二个是 perceived novelty，也就是玩家是否觉得这款游戏新鲜、有技术特色、不同于已有游戏。

## Slide 4: CONCEPTUAL BACKGROUND

Approx. time: 60 seconds

English:
The conceptual background is built around a tension. On the one hand, AI disclosure can reduce source-related value. For many creative products, people value the object partly because they believe it contains human effort, intention, and craft. If GenAI is disclosed, that belief may be weakened. On the other hand, games are experience-oriented and interactive. In this context, GenAI may also signal adaptive dialogue, dynamic events, or responsive world behavior. That can make the game seem more novel. So instead of assuming one overall negative disclosure effect, we propose a dual-pathway framework: a negative pathway through lower perceived effort and a positive pathway through higher perceived novelty.

中文：
理论背景的核心是一种张力。一方面，AI disclosure 可能削弱与来源相关的价值判断。对于很多创意产品，消费者之所以赋予价值，是因为他们相信产品中包含了人类努力、创作意图和手工投入。如果披露 GenAI 参与，这种信念可能被削弱。另一方面，游戏是体验导向、互动性的产品。在这个情境中，GenAI 也可能意味着自适应对话、动态事件或更有响应性的游戏世界。这会让游戏显得更有新颖性。因此，我们并不假设 disclosure 只有一个总体负面效应，而是提出一个双路径框架：通过降低 perceived effort 形成负向路径，同时通过提高 perceived novelty 形成正向路径。

## Slide 5: Stage of GenAI Involvement

Approx. time: 55 seconds

English:
The next key distinction is the stage of GenAI involvement. We distinguish pre-interaction involvement from during-interaction involvement. In the pre-interaction condition, GenAI is described as helping create content before play starts, such as dialogue, story events, and world content. This sounds more like a backstage production aid. In the during-interaction condition, GenAI is described as generating content in real time during gameplay. This sounds more like an in-play interaction capability. Our argument is that this distinction should matter especially for novelty. During-interaction involvement is more likely to make players imagine a dynamic and responsive experience.

中文：
接下来一个关键区分是 GenAI 参与的阶段。我们区分了 pre-interaction involvement 和 during-interaction involvement。在 pre-interaction 条件下，GenAI 被描述为在游戏开始前帮助创作内容，比如对话、故事事件和世界内容。这更像是后台生产工具。在 during-interaction 条件下，GenAI 被描述为在游玩过程中实时生成内容。这更像是游戏内的互动能力。我们的判断是，这一区分尤其会影响 perceived novelty。during-interaction 的表述更容易让玩家想象一种动态、响应式的游戏体验。

## Slide 6: THEORETICAL FRAMEWORK AND HYPOTHESES

Approx. time: 55 seconds

English:
This figure summarizes the research model. GenAI disclosure affects two perceptions. First, it can lower perceived effort. Second, it can increase perceived novelty. The stage of GenAI involvement moderates the disclosure-to-novelty path, because novelty should be more strongly activated when GenAI is framed as part of gameplay rather than only part of production. Both perceived effort and perceived novelty then feed into purchase intention. So the model is not saying that disclosure is simply good or bad. It asks which inference becomes more salient.

中文：
这张图总结了本文的研究模型。GenAI disclosure 会影响两个感知。第一，它可能降低 perceived effort。第二，它可能提高 perceived novelty。GenAI 参与阶段会调节 disclosure 到 novelty 的路径，因为当 GenAI 被表述为游戏过程的一部分，而不只是生产过程的一部分时，新颖性感知应该更容易被激活。随后，perceived effort 和 perceived novelty 都会影响 purchase intention。所以这个模型并不是简单地说 disclosure 好或者不好，而是在问：哪一种推断在玩家心中更突出。

## Slide 7: Hypotheses in One Sentence

Approx. time: 50 seconds

English:
The hypotheses follow directly from this model. H1 predicts that GenAI disclosure lowers perceived effort. H2 predicts that perceived effort increases purchase intention. H3 predicts that GenAI disclosure increases perceived novelty. H5 predicts that perceived novelty increases purchase intention. H4 is the boundary condition: during-interaction involvement should strengthen the novelty route. I separate H4 here because it is not simply between H3 and H5. It specifically concerns when the novelty mechanism becomes stronger.

中文：
这些假设直接来自前面的模型。H1 预测 GenAI disclosure 会降低 perceived effort。H2 预测 perceived effort 会提高 purchase intention。H3 预测 GenAI disclosure 会提高 perceived novelty。H5 预测 perceived novelty 会提高 purchase intention。H4 是边界条件：during-interaction involvement 会增强 novelty 这条路径。我这里把 H4 单独讲，是因为它并不是简单夹在 H3 和 H5 中间，而是专门说明 novelty 机制在什么情况下更强。

## Slide 8: RESEARCH METHOD

Approx. time: 45 seconds

English:
To test the model, we conducted a scenario-based online experiment in the context of digital games. The design had three conditions: no disclosure, pre-interaction disclosure, and during-interaction disclosure. We collected 399 valid responses, with 133 participants in each condition. In the analysis, we used mean comparisons, regression models with controls, and bootstrap mediation with 5,000 resamples. This design allows us to isolate the wording of the GenAI disclosure while keeping the focal product constant.

中文：
为了检验模型，我们在数字游戏情境下进行了一个基于情境材料的在线实验。实验包括三个条件：无披露、pre-interaction disclosure 和 during-interaction disclosure。最终获得 399 份有效样本，每个条件 133 人。分析中，我们使用均值比较、包含控制变量的回归模型，以及 5,000 次重抽样的 bootstrap mediation。这个设计的目的，是在保持产品信息不变的情况下，隔离 GenAI disclosure 表述方式的影响。

## Slide 9: Stimulus and Procedure

Approx. time: 55 seconds

English:
The procedure was straightforward. Participants imagined browsing a digital game platform. They then viewed a product page for a premium narrative role-playing game. Across conditions, the title, genre, price, screenshots, and product description were held constant. What changed was only the GenAI disclosure statement. In one condition, there was no AI statement. In the pre-interaction condition, GenAI was described as helping before gameplay. In the during-interaction condition, GenAI was described as contributing during gameplay. Importantly, this manipulation changes the disclosed stage of contribution, not the timing of disclosure presentation.

中文：
实验流程比较直接。参与者想象自己正在浏览一个数字游戏平台，然后看到一款付费叙事角色扮演游戏的产品页面。三个条件中，游戏标题、类型、价格、截图和产品描述都保持一致。唯一变化的是 GenAI disclosure statement。一个条件没有 AI 相关说明；pre-interaction 条件中，GenAI 被描述为在游戏开始前帮助创作；during-interaction 条件中，GenAI 被描述为在游戏过程中参与生成。需要强调的是，操纵的是披露中描述的 GenAI 参与阶段，而不是 disclosure 出现的时间点。

## Slide 10: Measures and Checks

Approx. time: 45 seconds

English:
We measured four main outcomes and beliefs. Perceived effort captures whether participants thought substantial human effort was invested in the game. Perceived novelty captures whether the game seemed new, fresh, and exciting. Purchase intention is the main behavioral intention outcome, and willingness to pay is included as a supplementary monetary outcome. The key scales showed satisfactory reliability, especially effort, novelty, and purchase intention. We also included controls such as openness to technology, genre preference, and AI familiarity, and we checked both perceived AI use and perceived stage of involvement.

中文：
我们测量了四类主要变量。Perceived effort 反映参与者是否认为这款游戏投入了大量人类努力。Perceived novelty 反映他们是否认为游戏新颖、新鲜且令人兴奋。Purchase intention 是主要的行为意向结果变量，willingness to pay 则作为补充性的货币结果变量。主要量表的信度较好，尤其是 effort、novelty 和 purchase intention。我们还控制了技术开放性、游戏类型偏好和 AI 熟悉度，并检查参与者是否感知到 AI 使用以及 GenAI 参与阶段。

## Slide 11: RESULTS: Manipulation Checks

Approx. time: 40 seconds

English:
Before testing the hypotheses, we first checked whether the manipulation worked. The results were clear. Participants in the disclosure conditions perceived significantly more AI use than those in the no-disclosure condition, with F equal to 339.58 and p below .001. They also distinguished whether GenAI was described as being used before play or during gameplay. This is important because our theory depends on separating disclosure presence from the disclosed stage of involvement. The checks support that separation.

中文：
在检验假设之前，我们先看操纵是否成功。结果很清楚。披露条件下的参与者显著感知到更多 AI 使用，相比无披露条件，F 值为 339.58，p 小于 .001。同时，参与者也能够区分 GenAI 是被描述为在游戏前使用，还是在游戏过程中使用。这一点很重要，因为本文理论依赖于区分 disclosure presence 和 disclosed stage of involvement。操纵检验支持了这个区分。

## Slide 12: RESULTS: The Effort Pathway

Approx. time: 55 seconds

English:
Now I turn to the effort pathway, corresponding to H1 and H2. H1 was supported: GenAI disclosure lowered perceived effort compared with no disclosure. The effect was statistically significant, with Welch's t equal to -6.18 and p below .001. H2 was also supported: perceived effort positively predicted purchase intention. However, the indirect effect through effort was only marginal, with p equal to .053. So the negative source-related mechanism exists, but in this setting it is not the strongest pathway to purchase intention.

中文：
接下来是 effort 路径，对应 H1 和 H2。H1 得到支持：与无披露条件相比，GenAI disclosure 显著降低了 perceived effort。结果显著，Welch's t 为 -6.18，p 小于 .001。H2 也得到支持：perceived effort 正向预测 purchase intention。不过，通过 effort 的间接效应只是边际显著，p 为 .053。因此，负向的来源相关机制确实存在，但在这个情境中，它并不是影响购买意愿最强的路径。

## Slide 13: RESULTS: The Novelty Pathway

Approx. time: 55 seconds

English:
The novelty pathway tells the other side of the story. H3 was supported: GenAI disclosure increased perceived novelty relative to no disclosure. The effect was significant, with Welch's t equal to 2.88 and p equal to .004. H5 was also strongly supported: perceived novelty had a much stronger relationship with purchase intention than perceived effort, with r equal to .554 and regression beta equal to .409. This suggests that in digital games, GenAI disclosure can activate a positive technology-based inference, not only a negative production-based inference.

中文：
novelty 路径呈现的是另一面。H3 得到支持：相对于无披露，GenAI disclosure 提高了 perceived novelty。这个结果显著，Welch's t 为 2.88，p 为 .004。H5 也得到强支持：perceived novelty 与 purchase intention 的关系明显强于 perceived effort，相关系数为 .554，回归 beta 为 .409。这说明在数字游戏中，GenAI disclosure 不只会激活负向的生产来源推断，也可能激活正向的技术新颖性推断。

## Slide 14: RESULTS: Stage-Contingent Novelty

Approx. time: 65 seconds

English:
The next result is central to the paper. H4 asks whether the novelty pathway depends on the stage of GenAI involvement. The answer is yes. Novelty was significantly higher when GenAI was framed as contributing during gameplay rather than before gameplay. The Welch's t value was 2.68, with p equal to .008. The moderated mediation result points in the same direction: the indirect effect through novelty was significant mainly for the during-interaction disclosure condition, and the index of moderated mediation was positive with a confidence interval excluding zero. So the positive effect of disclosure is not automatic. It becomes stronger when disclosure helps players imagine a novel gameplay experience.

中文：
接下来的结果是本文的核心。H4 关注 novelty 路径是否取决于 GenAI 参与阶段。答案是肯定的。当 GenAI 被描述为在游戏过程中参与，而不是只在游戏前参与时，玩家感知到的新颖性显著更高。Welch's t 为 2.68，p 为 .008。moderated mediation 的结果也指向同一结论：通过 novelty 的间接效应主要在 during-interaction disclosure 条件下显著，moderated mediation index 为正，并且置信区间不包含 0。因此，disclosure 的正向作用并不是自动发生的。它在 disclosure 能帮助玩家想象一种新颖的游戏体验时更强。

## Slide 15: Supplementary Outcome: Willingness to Pay

Approx. time: 40 seconds

English:
We also examined willingness to pay as a supplementary outcome. Here the pattern is weaker. We did not find significant condition-level differences in willingness to pay, and the distribution was highly skewed. The median willingness to pay was CNY 68 in all three conditions. At the mechanism level, the effort path was significantly negative, while the novelty path was positive but not significant. So for this study, purchase intention provides the clearer test of the proposed dual-pathway framework.

中文：
我们还补充考察了 willingness to pay。这个结果相对更弱。不同实验条件之间的 willingness to pay 没有显著差异，而且分布高度偏态。三个条件的中位数都是 68 元人民币。在机制层面，通过 effort 的路径显著为负，而通过 novelty 的路径为正但不显著。因此，在这项研究中，purchase intention 是更清晰地检验双路径框架的结果变量。

## Slide 16: DISCUSSION

Approx. time: 55 seconds

English:
The overall message is that GenAI disclosure is not a uniform negative cue. It does reduce perceived human effort, which can hurt purchase intention. But it also increases perceived novelty, which can improve purchase intention. More importantly, the positive route is strongest when GenAI is framed as part of gameplay. This helps explain why consumers may react differently to different GenAI disclosures. They are not only responding to the presence of AI. They are also interpreting what kind of role AI is expected to play.

中文：
总体来说，本文想传达的信息是：GenAI disclosure 并不是一个统一的负向线索。它确实会降低 perceived human effort，从而可能损害购买意愿。但它也会提高 perceived novelty，从而可能提升购买意愿。更重要的是，当 GenAI 被表述为游戏过程的一部分时，这条正向路径最强。这有助于解释为什么消费者会对不同 GenAI disclosure 产生不同反应。他们回应的不只是 AI 是否存在，而是在理解 AI 被期待扮演什么角色。

## Slide 17: Theoretical Contributions

Approx. time: 55 seconds

English:
The paper makes three theoretical contributions. First, it extends GenAI disclosure research from relatively static creative products to interactive hedonic systems. Second, it refines the concept of disclosure by distinguishing the presence of disclosure from the stage of GenAI involvement described in that disclosure. Third, it identifies perceived novelty as a positive mechanism that can coexist with effort-based discounting. This dual-pathway view gives us a more balanced understanding of GenAI disclosure in digital games.

中文：
本文有三个理论贡献。第一，它将 GenAI disclosure 研究从相对静态的创意产品扩展到互动性的享乐系统，也就是数字游戏。第二，它细化了 disclosure 的概念，区分了 disclosure 是否存在，以及 disclosure 中描述的 GenAI 参与阶段。第三，它识别了 perceived novelty 这一正向机制，并说明它可以与基于 effort 的负向折价机制共存。这种双路径视角让我们更平衡地理解数字游戏中的 GenAI disclosure。

## Slide 18: Practical Implications

Approx. time: 50 seconds

English:
The practical implication is that firms should not treat all GenAI disclosures as equivalent. Disclosure wording matters. If GenAI is described only as backstage automation, players may focus on reduced human input. But if GenAI is accurately described as supporting in-play value, such as dynamic dialogue or responsive events, it may activate expectations of novelty. The point is not to exaggerate AI capabilities. Rather, the point is to make the role of GenAI clear so that players can form the right expectation about the product experience.

中文：
实践启示是，企业不应该把所有 GenAI disclosure 都视为等价。披露措辞很重要。如果 GenAI 只被描述为后台自动化工具，玩家可能会更关注人类投入减少。但如果 GenAI 被准确描述为支持游戏内价值，比如动态对话或响应式事件，它可能激活玩家对新颖体验的期待。这里并不是说要夸大 AI 能力，而是要清楚说明 GenAI 的角色，让玩家对产品体验形成正确预期。

## Slide 19: Limitations and Future Research

Approx. time: 45 seconds

English:
There are also several limitations. First, the study focuses on the stage of disclosed GenAI involvement and does not examine other potentially important perceptions, such as quality, authenticity, or realism. Second, the experiment uses a single game genre and a single product-page scenario, which may limit generalizability. Third, although we included several controls, future research could examine whether individual differences in AI attitudes or innovation orientation further condition the observed effects.

中文：
本文也有一些局限。第一，研究聚焦于披露中的 GenAI 参与阶段，但没有检验其他可能重要的感知，例如 perceived quality、authenticity 或 realism。第二，实验使用的是单一游戏类型和单一产品页面情境，这可能限制结果的泛化性。第三，虽然我们纳入了若干控制变量，但未来研究可以进一步考察 AI attitudes 或 innovation orientation 等个体差异是否会调节这些效应。
